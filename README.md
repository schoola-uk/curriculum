# Schoola — UK Primary Curriculum

> Free, curriculum-aligned learning resources for UK primary school children (Years 1–6)

## Overview

**Schoola** is a free, independent educational signposting tool that maps the entire **England National Curriculum** (Key Stage 1 and Key Stage 2) and curates links to the best free educational resources available online.

### ✅ What Schoola Does
- Maps **all 12 core subjects** across Years 1–6
- Links to **free video lessons** (BBC Teach, Oak National Academy, BBC Bitesize)
- Provides **printable worksheets** from Twinkl and other trusted sources
- Offers **interactive quizzes** for self-assessment
- **No login required** — completely free and open access
- Fully aligned with the **UK National Curriculum**

### ❌ What Schoola Doesn't Do
Scholla does **not** create, host, or edit any content. We are a **discovery and signposting service** — think of us as a curated index to help parents and educators find quality free resources that already exist.

## How to Use

1. **Select your child's year group** (KS1: Years 1–2, or KS2: Years 3–6)
2. **Choose a subject** from the 12 available
3. **Find a topic** within that subject
4. **Access resources**:
   - 📺 Watch a free video lesson
   - 🖨️ Print a worksheet
   - ❓ Take a quiz to check understanding

All resources open in new tabs and are hosted by their original publishers (BBC, Oak Academy, Twinkl, etc.). Parents can feel confident these are established, quality providers.
